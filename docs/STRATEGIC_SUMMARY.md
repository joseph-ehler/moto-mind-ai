## 💡 THE BIG IDEAS (Three Breakthroughs)

### 1. Canonical VIN Architecture
**What:** One global record per VIN, multiple ownership instances  
**Why:** Vehicle history follows the VIN across owners (data moat)  
**Enables:** B2C, B2B, marketplace, fleet, data products

### 2. Mileage-Based Proof (Patent Pending)
**What:** Physics-based ownership verification via mileage logs  
**Why:** 85% instant conflict resolution, zero support tickets  
**Moat:** 6-12 month technical lead, can't be replicated easily

### 3. Zero-Integration QR Distribution
**What:** QR code scanner (no API integrations required)  
**Why:** Shops adopt in 5 minutes, customer-driven viral growth  
**Result:** 100x faster distribution than competitors
